Business Model Canvas

Cortex turns ad creation into a testable workflow.

The current buildathon version uses TRIBE v2 as a non-commercial proof of concept. A future paid version needs permission, a research partner, or a proprietary model.

EurHackNL May 2026 Student buildathon version
Key Partners
  • University mentors and neuroscience advisors
  • TRIBE v2 and Meta FAIR research materials for the proof of concept
  • OpenNeuro and Human Connectome Project data for future model work
  • Cloud GPU providers for training and inference
  • Marketing clubs, student founders, and small brands for feedback
Key Activities
  • Generate text, image, video, and audio ad ideas
  • Analyze text, audio, and video with TRIBE v2 in the demo
  • Turn neural scores into simple creative feedback
  • Export results for a presentation or campaign review
Value Proposition
  • Create ads and get early feedback before spending money.
  • Compare creative ideas using attention, emotion, memory, language, and overall score signals
  • Give small teams a faster alternative to guessing from opinions alone
  • No lab hardware needed for the demo workflow
  • A future proprietary model would make the commercial version stronger
Customer Relationships
  • Self-serve demo
  • Student team onboarding
  • Small pilot projects
  • Simple exportable reports
Customer Segments
  • Student founders
  • University marketing clubs
  • Small businesses testing social ads
  • Creators and early-stage teams
  • Marketing agencies as a later segment
Key Resources
  • Cortex web app and dashboard
  • Generated ad examples and saved results
  • TRIBE v2 demo connection
  • Team skills in frontend, backend, and AI APIs
  • Future model rights or research partnership
Channels
  • Buildathon demo
  • University network
  • Marketing and entrepreneurship clubs
  • LinkedIn posts and founder outreach
Cost Structure
  • GPU or API costs for neural analysis
  • LLM and media generation APIs
  • Hosting and database costs
  • Model training experiments if we continue
  • Design, engineering, and user testing time
  • Legal help for any paid version
Revenue Streams
  • Monthly student or small team plans
  • Pay per ad analysis
  • Paid pilot projects for small brands
  • Exported creative reports
  • Future API access
License note: TRIBE v2 is useful for a non-commercial buildathon proof of concept. It should not be presented as a ready-to-sell commercial engine. If Cortex continues after the event, the next step is to secure permission, work with a research partner, or train a model that can be used commercially.
